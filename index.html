Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?
For regular restaurants, 1-2 weeks is usually sufficient. For Michelin-starred or highly popular spots, we recommend 1-2 months in advance. Some exclusive omakase counters book up 3+ months ahead.
What if the restaurant is fully booked?
We'll suggest similar alternatives that match your preferences. You're never charged unless we secure a confirmed reservation.
Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?
Yes! We communicate your dietary needs directly to the restaurant in Japanese. We handle vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, and allergy requirements.
When do I pay?
You only pay our service fee after we confirm your reservation. Restaurant payment is made directly at the restaurant after your meal.
What's your cancellation policy?
Our service fee is non-refundable once a reservation is confirmed. However, we can help you modify or reschedule if the restaurant allows it.
