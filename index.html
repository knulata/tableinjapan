Questions & Answers

How are you so much cheaper than other services?
Other services charge per-person fees and add markups to meal prices. We charge a simple flat fee for the booking itself. We're not trying to make money on your meal — just on helping you get the reservation.
What if the restaurant is fully booked?
We'll suggest similar alternatives that match your preferences. You're never charged unless we secure a confirmed reservation. If we can't book anything you're happy with, full refund.
How far in advance should I book?
For most restaurants, 1-2 weeks is enough. For very popular spots or weekends, 3-4 weeks is better. Let us know your dates and we'll tell you what's realistic.
Can you handle dietary restrictions?
Yes! We communicate your needs directly to the restaurant in Japanese. Vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, allergies — we handle it all.
When and how do I pay?
You pay our booking fee only after we confirm your reservation. We accept PayPal. Restaurant payment is made directly at the restaurant after your meal.
What's your cancellation policy?
Our booking fee is non-refundable once confirmed, as we've already secured and guaranteed your spot. However, we can help you reschedule if the restaurant allows it.
